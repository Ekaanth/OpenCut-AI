## Descript Is Great. The Price Tag Isn't.

Descript popularized text-based video editing — the idea that you can edit video by editing a transcript. It's brilliant. But Descript's pricing isn't:

- **Free plan:** 1 hour of transcription, 720p exports, limited features
- **Hobby plan:** $24/month per user
- **Pro plan:** $33/month per user
- **Business plan:** $65/month per user

For a team of 3 editors, that's $72–195/month. Every month. Forever.

**OpenCut AI gives you Descript's best features — and more — for free.** No trial, no freemium, no credit card. Just free, open-source video editing with AI.

## Feature-by-Feature: OpenCut AI vs Descript

### Text-Based Editing

**Descript:** Edit video by editing the transcript. Delete text to delete video. This is Descript's signature feature and the main reason people pay for it.

**OpenCut AI:** Same feature. Completely free. Transcribe your video, then delete sentences, drag paragraphs to reorder, remove individual words. The timeline updates automatically.

**Verdict:** Tie on features. OpenCut AI wins on price ($0 vs $24+/month).

### AI Transcription

**Descript:** Built-in transcription with speaker detection. 1 hour free, then you pay.

**OpenCut AI:** Whisper-powered transcription with word-level timestamps. Unlimited. Free. Works offline.

**Verdict:** OpenCut AI wins. No usage limits, no hourly caps, and it works without internet.

### Filler Word Removal

**Descript:** "Remove filler words" detects "um", "uh", "like" and removes them. Available on paid plans.

**OpenCut AI:** Same feature. Detects filler words, highlights them in the transcript, one-click removal. Free.

**Verdict:** OpenCut AI wins. Free vs paid.

### Voice Cloning and TTS

**Descript:** Not available. Descript has stock AI voices but no voice cloning from a sample.

**OpenCut AI:** Clone any voice from a 6-second sample. Generate voiceovers in the cloned voice. Support for 22 Indian languages and 15+ international languages. Free.

**Verdict:** OpenCut AI wins. Descript doesn't have this feature at all.

### Subtitles and Captions

**Descript:** Auto-generate captions from transcript. Style customization on paid plans.

**OpenCut AI:** One-click subtitle generation with karaoke, pill, and classic styles. Speaker-labeled captions with color coding. YouTube chapters export. Free.

**Verdict:** OpenCut AI wins on features and price.

### Screen Recording

**Descript:** Built-in screen recording with webcam overlay. Record, edit, and publish in one tool.

**OpenCut AI:** Not built in (yet). Use OBS Studio (free) or your built-in screen recorder, then edit in OpenCut AI.

**Verdict:** Descript wins on convenience. But OBS + OpenCut AI is still $0 total.

### Collaboration

**Descript:** Real-time collaboration on paid plans. Comments, shared workspaces, team features.

**OpenCut AI:** Share project links with expiration and password protection. Full real-time collaboration is on the roadmap.

**Verdict:** Descript wins today for teams. OpenCut AI is better for solo creators.

### Privacy

**Descript:** All your footage is uploaded to Descript's cloud servers. Their privacy policy states they may process your content for AI improvements.

**OpenCut AI:** Everything runs on your machine. No uploads. No cloud processing. No data collection. Open-source and auditable.

**Verdict:** OpenCut AI wins for privacy.
