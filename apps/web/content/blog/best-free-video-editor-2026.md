## How to Get Started for Free

1. **Clone the repo:** `git clone https://github.com/Ekaanth/OpenCut-AI.git`
2. **Copy the env file:** `cp apps/web/.env.example apps/web/.env.local`
3. **Start the backend:** `docker compose up -d`
4. **Install and run:** `bun install && bun dev:web`
5. **Open your browser:** Go to `http://localhost:3000`

That's it. No credit card. No account creation. No email required. Just free video editing with AI.
