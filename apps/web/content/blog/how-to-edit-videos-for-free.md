## Step 3: Basic Editing — Cut, Trim, and Split

### Cutting clips

1. Click on a clip in the timeline
2. Move the playhead (the white vertical line) to where you want to cut
3. Press **Ctrl+B** (or **Cmd+B** on Mac) to split the clip
4. Delete the part you don't want with the Delete key

### Trimming clips

Drag the left or right edge of a clip to shorten it. This is the fastest way to remove unwanted footage from the beginning or end.

### Ripple delete

When you delete a clip, the gap closes automatically. No manual shuffling needed. This is called "ripple trim" and it saves enormous time on longer projects.

## Free Video Editing Tips for Beginners

### Tip 1: Use Keyboard Shortcuts

Learn these 5 shortcuts and you'll edit 3x faster:

- **Space** — play / pause
- **Ctrl+Z** — undo
- **Ctrl+B** — split clip at playhead
- **J / K / L** — rewind / pause / fast-forward (press multiple times for speed)
- **M** — drop a marker

### Tip 2: Edit the Transcript, Not the Timeline

If your video has speech, use text-based editing. Open the transcript, delete the sentences you don't want, and the video edits itself. This is 10x faster than manual cutting.

### Tip 3: Use Templates

OpenCut AI includes 8 free project templates: YouTube Intro, TikTok Vlog, Podcast Highlight, Product Review, Instagram Reel, and more. Start with a template and customize from there.

### Tip 4: Normalize Your Audio

Use the **LUFS Loudness Normalization** feature to make your audio sound professional. One click targets YouTube (-14 LUFS), Spotify (-14 LUFS), or Apple Podcasts (-16 LUFS) loudness standards.

### Tip 5: Add Transitions Between Clips

OpenCut AI includes 20 free transitions: cross-dissolve, dip-black, slide, wipe, zoom, glitch, film burn, and more. Drag and drop between clips. All WebGL-powered, all real-time.
