## How Free Voice Cloning Works

### Step 1: Upload a Voice Sample

Record or upload a clean audio sample of the voice you want to clone. **6 seconds is enough.** The clearer the sample (no background noise, no music), the better the clone.

### Step 2: The AI Learns the Voice

OpenCut AI uses **XTTS v2** (Coqui's open-source text-to-speech model) to analyze the voice sample and create a voice profile. This process takes seconds and runs entirely on your local machine.

### Step 3: Generate Voiceovers

Type or paste your script. The AI generates speech in the cloned voice:

- Natural intonation and pacing
- Emotion and emphasis matching the original voice
- Per-sentence audio clips aligned to your timeline

All free. All local. No data sent to any cloud.
